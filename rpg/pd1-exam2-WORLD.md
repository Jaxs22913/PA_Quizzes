# The Long Survey — World Design (PD1 Exam 2)

**Genre:** post-apocalyptic. **Structure:** one continuous ruined region the player descends through, top to bottom — from the sunlit ridge down into the drowned undercity. Nine districts, one per PD exam topic, in head-to-toe examination order. A single main road (the route the Surveyor walks) threads all nine, sealed between districts by boss-gates that open only when that district's warlord falls. Terrain (cliffs, flooded galleries, sludge channels, a black cistern) shapes each route and justifies the collision map.

Legend for the schematic: blue dot = player start · red = enemy (staged by fiction) · orange = campfire (one per district, before the boss) · yellow = chest (side area) · magenta = boss (at the gate) · gold band = boss-locked gate to the next district.

---

### 1. Vantage Rise — *General Survey & Vital Signs*
The last high ground before the sprawl. **Sub-locations:** the **Observation Deck** (upper-left ruin, where you first survey the whole region), the **Watch Station** (upper-right), and **The Checkpoint** (the gate down). A switchback ridge road drops from the deck to the checkpoint. **Encounters:** two lookouts flank the road from the deck's shadow and the switchback (they're *counting* everyone who passes — the twist); a patrol paces the ledge. **Campfire** in a sheltered nook off the road; **chest** stashed inside the observation deck. **Boss:** the Warden holds the checkpoint gate itself.

### 2. The Tannery Rows — *Skin, Hair & Nails*
A shanty market street of patched tarps and drying hides. **Sub-locations:** **Tanner Shops** and the **Drying Racks** shed (left), **The Hide Market** (right, the Baron's lair). A central market street funnels everyone past the stalls. **Encounters:** two guards form a chokepoint down the market street; a toll-taker lurks in the drying-racks shed. **Campfire** at a quiet stall at the street's end; **chest** in the back of the shed. **Boss:** the Patchwork Baron at the hide-market gate.

### 3. Echo Hollow — *HENT (Head, Ears, Nose, Throat)*
A narrowing ravine pass walled by cliffs, lined with leaning broadcast towers. **Sub-locations:** **The Leaning Towers**, **The Narrows** (a 2-wide throat chokepoint), **The Crier's Perch** at the head of the pass. The floor winds and pinches as you climb. **Encounters:** an ambusher wedged in the Narrows; a wanderer pacing between towers; a sentry under the last tower. **Campfire** at a wide sheltered bend; **chest** in a cliff alcove. **Boss:** the Crier broadcasting from the perch.

### 4. Lantern Watch — *Eye*
A ruined lighthouse yard built on ground glass. **Sub-locations:** the **Glass Foundry** (left), the **Lens Works** (right), and **The Great Lens** tower (center, the Lamplighter's post). Ring-paths circle the tower base. **Encounters:** foundry and lens-works crews at their benches; a sentry circling the tower on the ring path. **Campfire** in a sheltered yard corner; **chest** in a locked lens-works drawer. **Boss:** the Lamplighter atop the great lens.

### 5. The Bellows Yards — *Lung & Thorax*
An industrial air-works — a maze of vent walls feeding great stacks. **Sub-locations:** **The Vent Maze** (upper working yard), **The Stacks** (lower), **Master Bellows** (center-bottom, the Stoker's lair). **Encounters:** a press-gang crew clusters where the vent maze pinches; an overseer patrols the upper vents; a crew hauls near the right stacks. **Campfire** in a quiet dead-end vent out of the smoke; **chest** behind the right-hand furnace. **Boss:** the Stoker at the master bellows.

### 6. The Pump Works — *Cardiovascular & Peripheral Vascular*
The half-drowned waterworks. Most of the district is **open water**; movement is over **pipe-bridges** — a top gallery walk, two cross-bridges, and a central spine bridge. **Sub-locations:** **The Flooded Galleries**, **Pipe Bridges**, **The Main Junction** (a dry hall, the Valvemaster's post). **Encounters:** guards hold the cross-bridge chokepoints over open water; a patrol walks the central spine. **Campfire** on a dry landing above the junction; **chest** on an isolated side platform across the water. **Boss:** the Valvemaster where every pipe meets.

### 7. The Provision Halls — *Abdominal*
The granary quarter. **Sub-locations:** **The Silos** (four across the top), **Storehouse Row** (long buildings left and right), **The Mills** (center, the Miller's grain-wheel). A market/mill lane runs down the middle. **Encounters:** two guards seal the storehouse doors (hoarding the stores — the twist); an overseer works the market floor. **Campfire** in a gap between silos; **chest** a sealed crate deep in the left storehouse. **Boss:** the Miller atop the grain-wheel.

### 8. The Outflow — *Anus & Rectum*
The drainage works — sludge channels (hazard water) crossed by sluice-gate walkways. **Sub-locations:** **The Sludge Channels**, **The Sluice Gates** (two crossing walkways), **The Outfall** control (the Sluicewarden's gate). Service walks run beside the channels. **Encounters:** a gatekeeper on the upper sluice crossing; a wanderer on a service walk; a guard on the lower crossing. **Campfire** on a dry right-side ledge; **chest** at a dead-end side outfall. **Boss:** the Sluicewarden at the last gate to the depths.

### 9. The Deep Cistern — *Male GU & Prostate* (final)
The bottom of everything: a vast drowned chamber of still black water. A **drowned stair** descends in; a narrow **causeway** snakes over deep water to the central **Wellspring** platform (the Reclaimer's arena). **Encounters:** three elite-feeling guards posted at the causeway's doglegs and the final approach. **Campfire** on a dry landing at the stair's foot; **chest** on a submerged side ledge reached by a thin spur. **Boss:** the Reclaimer on the wellspring causeway — the final boss, whose wrong-answer-regenerates-a-segment twist is announced in his pre-boss beat.

---

**Density follows the story:** exactly 3 staged enemies per district (27 total), placed at chokepoints, patrols, and lairs — never evenly scattered. Empty stretches (the open ridge, the water, the ravine floor) are left empty on purpose. One campfire and one chest per district, both off the main line so they're a deliberate detour, not handed to you.
