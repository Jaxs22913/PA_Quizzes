## Question-structure convention (as found)

Physical Diagnosis questions are framed around the physical examination itself, not physiology mechanisms: proper technique, patient positioning, exam sequence/order, equipment use (otoscope, ophthalmoscope, stethoscope, sphygmomanometer), the inspection/palpation/percussion/auscultation methods and what they assess, exam-relevant surface landmarks, and normal-versus-abnormal findings on exam. Confirmed by reading ~12 samples across all nine topics: every stem is procedure- or finding-oriented (e.g., otoscope speculum selection, Weber/Rinne interpretation, chest-expansion thumb placement, DRE sphincter-tone maneuver), with four plausible exam-technique options and each wrong option carrying a rationale explaining why that specific technique or interpretation is incorrect. New items match this style and lean applied/vignette where the content supports it.
