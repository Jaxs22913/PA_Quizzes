Before you begin

Seventy questions, shaped like the real exam as the course described it: a couple of rhythm strips, and a much larger share of patient cases that describe a heart rhythm in words (rate, regularity, P waves, PR interval, QRS width) and then ask for the diagnosis or its cause, the right test, or the next step in management.

Twenty-eight of the seventy are rhythm questions (three strips in every form); the other forty-two come from venous disorders, arterial disease and aneurysms, cardiomyopathy, pericardial disease and infective endocarditis. Fifty-six are patient vignettes.

These forms favor questions you have not met in the 60-question master exams. Four options, A–D, every wrong choice explained, and every question cites the slide it came from.
