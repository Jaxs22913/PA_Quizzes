Before you begin

Built from Professor Webster’s own Exam 2 review at the end of the Epidemiology lecture. Every question is new and written for one item of that review: each of her items gets one question in each set, the two on different facts, and the items she put to the class as questions (know cold) get extra ones. What she said not to memorize, and the organisms she said not to worry about, are left out. Every fact comes from her slides, cited under each answer.

Questions run in her order: Disorders in Immunity, then the Gram-positive bacilli organism by organism, then Epidemiology, so the score breakdown matches the headings of the Webster review page.

Her review covered only her three lectures. Dr. Fair’s lectures (Diagnosing Infections, Cocci, Novel Antimicrobial Therapy, Miscellaneous Bacterial Agents) are not in these sets; they are in their own topic quizzes and the five master exams.
