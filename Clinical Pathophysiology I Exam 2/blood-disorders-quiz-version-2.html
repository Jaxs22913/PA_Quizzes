Before you begin

Mechanism only, which is the line this course draws against Clinical Medicine and Surgery: why the blood fails belongs here, what is done about it belongs there. Ask where the problem sits in the lineage. Every circulating cell starts from one stem cell and splits into myeloid and lymphoid branches, so the cell lines that are affected point to where the defect arises. Lymphoid neoplasms are sorted by lineage and stage: precursor (acute lymphoblastic leukemia), mature (chronic lymphocytic leukemia) or differentiated (plasma-cell myeloma). Sort every anemia two ways: by size (microcytic below 80, normocytic 80 to 100, macrocytic above 100 femtoliters) and by mechanism — loss, destruction or diminished production.
