Before you begin

Thirty questions on reading a rhythm strip and the rhythms that come from the sinus node, and about half of them show you the strip. The first part reviews the foundation the rhythms rest on: the conduction system and its three pacemakers, the action potential, the paper, every wave and interval, and where the leads go. Then the strips: count the rate, decide whether it is regular, check the P waves and the PR interval, look at the QRS, and name the rhythm. Normal sinus rhythm, sinus bradycardia and sinus tachycardia differ only in rate; sinus arrhythmia only in regularity; sinus exit block, sinus pause and sinus arrest by whether the missing beats march out and how many are missed.
