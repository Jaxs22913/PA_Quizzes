Histamine Receptor Actions

Where the H1 and H2 receptors are and what histamine does at each, the reason antihistamines do what they do.

Lecture 5 — Ear, Nose and Throat Drugs 9 rows

H1 blockers (the antihistamines on the comparison chart) act on the H1 rows: itch and pain, swelling from leaky venules, bronchial contraction. The H2 receptors run gastric acid secretion, which is why H2 blockers treat acid, not allergy. The slide puts heart rate on H1 and force of contraction on H2; some references put both on H2.
