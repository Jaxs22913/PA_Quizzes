What each drug is FOR, and what you tell the patient. 244 entries across the Exam 2 lectures, each citing its slide. Indications and patient education are the two things students under-study most.

Drug of choice — The slide says “drug of choice” (or first-line / preferred) for that indication.Indication — A stated indication or use.Education-heavy — The row where the counseling point matters more than the indication.Monitoring — A target level or laboratory goal.
