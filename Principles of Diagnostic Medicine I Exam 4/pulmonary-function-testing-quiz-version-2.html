Before you begin

Thirty questions on pulmonary function testing and oxygenation, and about a third of them show you a tracing, a loop, a film or a report. Know the equipment and what each test measures, the four lung volumes and four capacities, how spirometry is done and its forced values, why the tests are ordered, how obstruction and restriction differ on the curves and loops, how to read a report step by step, the role of each standard test, and how oxygenation is checked. Reports use FEV1 (forced expiratory volume in one second), FVC (forced vital capacity), TLC (total lung capacity) and DLCO (diffusing capacity of the lung for carbon monoxide).
