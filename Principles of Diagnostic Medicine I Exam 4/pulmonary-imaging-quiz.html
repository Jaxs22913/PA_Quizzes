Before you begin

Thirty questions on pulmonary imaging, and about a third of them show you the film or the scan. Find the normal structures and the bony landmarks, tell a technique problem (exposure, inspiration, rotation, the anteroposterior view) from real disease, pick the right study and know its cautions (radiograph, ultrasound, computed tomography with or without contrast, the angiogram, the ventilation/perfusion scan, positron emission tomography, magnetic resonance), and recognize pneumonia, pleural effusion, pulmonary edema, pneumothorax and interstitial lung disease.
