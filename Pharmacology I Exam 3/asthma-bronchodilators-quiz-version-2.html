Before you begin

Asthma is reversible airway narrowing driven by inflammation. The bronchodilators reverse bronchospasm: beta-2 agonists raise cyclic adenosine monophosphate (short-acting for relief; long-acting never alone in asthma), theophylline has a narrow therapeutic index, and the anticholinergics block muscarinic receptors. What these do and do not ask. Drug choice, indications, adverse effects, contraindications and patient education are in, weighted above mechanism. Doses are out (milligram amounts, fluid volumes, inhaler and nebulizer doses), and so are bare drug levels and the slide points that current guidelines no longer follow, which is why the sets run shorter than the usual 30.
