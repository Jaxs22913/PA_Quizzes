What Each Drug Does to Potassium

Potassium can kill a patient quickly, so every drug in this lecture is sorted by whether it lowers or raises it, why, and what that means when drugs are combined.

Lecture 9 — Diuretics and Heart Failure Drugs 8 rows

The combinations are the point. A patient may be on a loop diuretic (lowers potassium), an angiotensin-converting enzyme inhibitor (raises it) and a potassium-sparing drug or supplement (raises it); the job is to know where the patient will end up. A loop diuretic plus a potassium-sparing diuretic is a common deliberate pairing to offset potassium loss. (Added background: potassium chloride salt substitutes add potassium.)
