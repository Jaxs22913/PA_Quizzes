Before you begin

Sixty questions drawn from every topic in the Exam 1 block, in proportion rather than at random — all six contribute to every form, so this is a genuine cumulative rehearsal and not a sample of whichever topic had the most questions written for it. No question appears in more than one form, so working through all five gives you 300 distinct questions. Every question cites its slide. Weighted the way the course asked for: Dr. McInnis told the class that mechanism is over-studied, so indications, patient education, side effects and contraindications carry the count. No question turns on a drug dose, per Dr. Wood. This rebuild (v2, 30 August) folds in the new short clinical vignettes and is written to be more to the point — brief stems, and answer choices short enough to scan, with the reasoning moved into the explanation. The five v1 forms are kept under Archived quizzes for extra practice.
